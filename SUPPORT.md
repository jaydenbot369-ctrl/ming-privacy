## Common questions

### How do I delete my account?

In the app: **Settings → Delete Account**. This removes your account and all associated data from our servers. The action cannot be undone.

If you cannot reach the in-app option, email us at support@ming-app.com and we will delete your account within 30 days.

### How do I cancel my subscription or get a refund?

Subscriptions and purchases are managed by Apple. To cancel or request a refund:

1. Open **Settings → [Your Name] → Subscriptions** on your iPhone or iPad
2. Tap **Ming**
3. Choose **Cancel Subscription**

For refund requests, visit [reportaproblem.apple.com](https://reportaproblem.apple.com) and sign in with your Apple ID.

Ming cannot directly process refunds — only Apple can.

### Why is my reading taking a long time to generate?

Each reading is computed on demand. If it has been more than 30 seconds, check your internet connection, close the app, and reopen it. Your saved readings are preserved.

### Why does my reading look slightly different the second time?

Ming combines a deterministic chart calculation with a narrative layer. The calculation is stable; the narrative phrasing can vary. The underlying insights should remain consistent.

### How accurate is a Ming reading?

Ming is a symbolic reading tool for entertainment and self-reflection. Content is not a substitute for medical, psychological, legal, or financial advice.

### Is my data private?

Yes. See our [Privacy Policy](https://jaydenbot369-ctrl.github.io/ming-privacy/) for a full explanation of what we collect, why, and who it is shared with. We never sell or share your data with advertisers.

### I found a bug or have a feature request

Email us at support@ming-app.com with:
- What you were trying to do
- What happened
- What you expected to happen

We read every message.
